BREAKFAST: idli/dosa/pesarattu portions, add sambar or chutney with protein,
avoid sugary chai; eggs or curd on the side.
RICE: smaller portion, fill half the plate with vegetables, add dal or curd,
eat vegetables and protein first.
CHAI: reduce sugar gradually, prefer smaller cups, avoid biscuits with chai.
OFFICE LUNCH: home-packed rice + dal + sabzi, portion guide, fruit instead of
sweets.
WEEKEND: biryani/festival food portion and pairing guide, walk after meals.
MOVEMENT: 10-15 minute walk after meals as tolerated.
